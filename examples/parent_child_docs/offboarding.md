# Offboarding guide

## Before your last day

Tell your manager and HR in writing at least four weeks before your last day.
HR then sends you the exit form, which you fill in online. Hand over your open
work: write a short note for each project and share it with your team. Copy any
personal files off your laptop, because IT cannot restore them after the wipe.
Book a return slot for your equipment in the IT portal, under Leaving. Use up
your remaining leave days, or agree with HR that they are paid out with your
last salary.

## On your last day

Bring your laptop, monitor, headset and badge to your return slot. IT wipes the
laptop together with you and signs the return form. Your email and accounts are
closed at 18:00. Give your badge to reception on the way out.

## After you leave

Your final salary is paid with the next monthly salary run. A reference letter
is sent within two weeks if you ask HR for one. Your pension statement arrives
by post within three months.
