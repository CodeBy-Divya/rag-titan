# IT handbook

## Your laptop

New employees get a laptop on their first day. It comes with the company
software installed and is encrypted. Do not install software that IT has not
approved, and lock the screen whenever you leave your desk.

## Passwords

Use a password manager and a different password for every system. Passwords
need at least 14 characters. Turn on two-step login for email and the IT portal
in your first week.

## Getting help

Open a ticket in the IT portal for anything that does not work. Urgent problems,
such as a lost laptop, can be reported by phone at any time.
